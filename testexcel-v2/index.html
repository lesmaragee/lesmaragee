11 Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions

What does TestExcel do?

TestExcel is a South African technology company specialising in fintech and payments. We design and develop payment solutions, build custom software, integrate payment systems, and create AI-powered business automation. We also provide payments advisory and assurance, including scheme compliance testing, for teams that need specialist payments guidance.

Does TestExcel build fintech applications?

Yes. TestExcel designs and develops payment-enabled applications, transaction workflows, reporting platforms and digital financial solutions, drawing on deep payments domain knowledge to build software that handles real-world payment complexity correctly.

Can TestExcel integrate payment gateways?

Yes. TestExcel connects payment gateways, PSPs, APIs and business systems to enable reliable digital payment experiences, working across the customer checkout, authentication, processing and settlement layers of a payment journey.

Does TestExcel develop custom software for businesses?

Yes. TestExcel builds web applications, business platforms, dashboards, portals and custom software designed around real operational needs, for fintechs, banks and businesses that need software built around how they actually work.

How can AI automation help my business?

AI-powered automation can reduce manual work by connecting business tools, consolidating data from multiple systems, and turning operational information into reports and dashboards your team can act on, instead of hours spent on repetitive administrative tasks.

Can TestExcel build financial dashboards?

Yes. TestExcel builds dashboards that consolidate transactions, revenue, expenses and payment information into a single view, so business owners can monitor financial and operational performance without relying on multiple disconnected reports.

Does TestExcel provide payments advisory?

Yes. TestExcel provides specialist payments knowledge, technical guidance, integration assessments, scheme compliance testing and release readiness support for teams that need independent payments expertise alongside their own delivery.

Where is TestExcel based?

TestExcel is based in South Africa and works with fintechs, banks and businesses remotely across markets, supporting payment technology, software development and automation projects wherever they are delivered.
