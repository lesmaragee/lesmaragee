09 Frequently asked

Direct answers, no spin.

Does TestExcel perform formal card scheme certification?

No. TestExcel provides scheme compliance testing, validating payment changes against card scheme mandates and requirements before release. Formal certification is a separate process run by the card schemes themselves.

What is the difference between scheme compliance testing and scheme certification?

Compliance testing checks a payment change against scheme mandate requirements before submission. Certification is the formal sign-off issued by the schemes, which TestExcel does not perform.

Does TestExcel use AI in test automation?

Yes. TestExcel uses Devin AI to accelerate test creation, maintenance and regression coverage, with experienced QA judgment deciding what gets tested and why.

Is TestExcel an AI company?

No. TestExcel is a payments QA specialist. AI is used as an accelerator inside test automation, not as a replacement for payments expertise or QA judgment.

Where is TestExcel based, and which markets does it serve?

TestExcel is based in South Africa and works with banks, fintechs and payment providers across multiple markets, including remote engagements.

Does TestExcel only provide testers, or can it advise and train teams too?

Both. Alongside payments QA and scheme compliance testing, TestExcel offers QA advisory, test strategy and documentation, vendor and partner coordination, QA capability building, payments QA training and QA talent solutions.
